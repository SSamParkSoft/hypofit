# Product Philosophy

Status: service-source-of-truth

Last updated: 2026-07-02

## Mission

Hypofit helps founders validate real customer problems faster by recruiting
people who actually match the target customer segment. It gives respondents a
simple way to turn relevant lived experience into paid interview participation.

The service should feel like a practical coordination tool, not a research
dashboard. The primary value is reducing the friction between "I need to talk
to the right customers" and "I completed a useful interview."

## Core Value

For founders:

- Find real target customers quickly.
- State the customer segment and interview request clearly.
- Review applications with enough context to make a decision.
- Coordinate the interview without leaving the app too early.
- Reduce uncertainty around attendance and completion.

For respondents:

- See reward, time, location, mode, and fit before applying.
- Apply with relevant experience and available times.
- Chat with the founder after applying.
- Know the status of applications and interviews.
- Report unsafe, misleading, or inappropriate requests.

## Product Taste

Hypofit should be:

- Mobile-first for the actual interview workflow.
- Clear and restrained, with operational density over marketing decoration.
- Trust-oriented, because the product coordinates real meetings, money, and
  user-generated content.
- Fast to understand on a phone.
- Calm in error, empty, and permission states.

Hypofit should not feel like:

- A generic survey builder.
- A job marketplace.
- A social feed.
- A crypto/payment/reward guarantee product.
- An AI matching product before the manual workflow proves itself.

## MVP Learning Goals

The first release should answer:

- Are founders willing to pay or promise reward for real interviews?
- Are respondents willing to participate when the terms are clear?
- Do respondents care more about reward, distance, time, topic, or mode?
- Can founder screening work through simple application answers?
- Does chat reduce uncertainty after application?
- What needs to be tracked to prevent or handle no-shows?

## Product Boundaries

The MVP should keep product scope inside the interview loop. Features are
acceptable when they directly support:

- creating interview opportunities,
- browsing or searching opportunities,
- applying,
- coordinating,
- selecting/rejecting,
- completing,
- reporting/supporting,
- meeting store review and legal requirements.

Features should be challenged when they introduce:

- automated payment settlement,
- AI ranking/matching,
- transcription or recording,
- complex admin workflows,
- multi-tenant organization management,
- unrelated social/community behavior.
